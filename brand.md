# Brand Specification: Cloud Mining Clone

## Step 1: The Name

### Chosen Names: 10 Candidates (Will be narrowed to final 5 after trademark checks)

**Descriptive Style:**
- `Hashnest` - Suggests mining and community
- `Calcuda` - Modern, tech-forward
- `Minepool` - Direct reference to mining

**Compound Style:**
- `HashTrade` - Trading/buying crypto power
- `Craphouse` - Mining as industrial

**Invented Style:**
- `VirtuMiner` - Virtualization of mining
- `Stratum` - Mining infrastructure
- `Excavate` - Deeper mining

**Metaphor Style:**
- `CryptoNest` - Security and shelter
- `HashingHub` - Centralized mining hub

**Verb Style:**
- `Hackle` - To stake/win
- `Ravine` - To mine

### Naming Filters Applied:
1. **No confusion with original:** All names avoid "Bitdeer" sound/alphabetical similarity
2. **Trademark-friendly:** Created coined words and modified keywords
3. **Short & memorable:** 1-2 syllables, clear meaning
4. **Angle-aligned:** Focus on cloud mining control and simplicity

**Final 5 for Trademark Checks:**
1. **Hashnest** - Descriptive, modern, distinctive
2. **Calcuda** - Unique, tech-forward
3. **HashTrade** - Direct commercial intent
4. **VirtuMiner** - Innovative virtualization
5. **CryptoNest** - Secure mining sanctuary

## Step 4: Logo Brief

### Core Concept:
"A secure, approachable digital mining facility represented as an abstracted bitcoin symbol with roots of growth, suggesting trust and accessibility in cloud mining."

### Logo Type:
**Symbol + Wordmark** - Custom icon representing hashing/anchor + clean font

### Design Requirements:
- **Size Variations:** 16px (favicon) → 1024px (app icon)
- **iOS Specific:** Full color 1024x1024px, no transparency required
- **Social Media:** 1200x630px hero image with brand voice
- **No resemblance to Bitdeer:** Different core shape, color palette, and typographic approach

### Visual Check Protocol:
Original Bitdeer logo placed next to logo drafts to ensure no shared:
- Shape elements (geometric similarities)
- Color combinations (blue/gold vs orange/gold palette)
- Letterform tricks or design motifs

## Step 5: Voice Guide

### Three Word Persona:
1. **Empowered** - Confident, knowledgeable, in control
2. **Transparent** - Clear, honest, no hidden fees or jargon
3. **Accessible** - Welcoming, simple, for everyone

### Do/Don't Pairs:

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Explain complex concepts in simple terms | Use crypto jargon or industry acronyms |
| Provide clear pricing breakdowns | Hide fees in small print |
| Show real-time mining progress | Use vague "active mining" descriptions |
| Offer 24/7 support access | Just have an email address |
| Celebrate milestones | Be formal and distant |

### Voice Rewrites (Current Clone Strings Updated):

**Sign Up:**
- Old: "Create an account" → **New: "Start mining today"**
- Old: "Sign up" → **New: "Begin your mining journey"**

**Main Button (Explore Plans):**
- Old: "Start Mining" → **New: "Select your plan"**
- Old: "Explore Plans" → **New: "Choose your contract"**

**Empty States:**
- Old: "No active contracts" → **New: "No mining contracts yet - let's start"**
- Old: "No orders" → **New: "Your mining contracts will appear here"**

**Error Messages:**
- Old: "Insufficient balance" → **New: "Your balance doesn't cover this contract"**
- Old: "Payment failed" → **New: "Unable to process payment - check your balance"**

**Confirmation Messages:**
- Old: "Payment successful" → **New: "Your contract is active! Mining has started"**
- Old: "Order placed" → **New: "Contract activated successfully"**
