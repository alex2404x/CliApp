# Launch Plan: Hashnest

## 1. Pre-Launch Phase
- **Waitlist/Beta Program:** Set up a simple waitlist landing page to capture interest from cryptocurrency communities.
- **Analytics & Error Tracking:** Integrate PostHog or Sentry for real-time monitoring.
- **Beta Users:** Onboard 10-20 active miners for a closed beta to gather feedback on daily payout mechanics and UI clarity.

## 2. Launch Strategy
- **Product Hunt:** Lead with the fix for transparency in cloud mining fees and instant activation.
- **Hacker News:** Focus on the tech stack (Next.js 14, Supabase RLS) and the idempotent reward distribution engine.
- **Community Outreach:** Target specific Reddit threads (r/CloudMining, r/BitcoinMining) and specialized forums where users have complained about "Bitdeer complexity" or "hidden fees."

## 3. Targeted Channels for First 10 Users
Identify and reach out to users in these communities who have expressed frustration with traditional mining platforms:
1. **Reddit r/CloudMining** - Active discussion on fees and hardware costs.
2. **Twitter/X Mining Communities** - Influencers discussing accessible entry points.
3. **BitcoinTalk Forums** - Mining technical discussions and platform comparisons.
4. **Discord Mining Hubs** - Real-time troubleshooting and recommendations.
5. **Mining YouTube Channel Comment Sections** - Users asking for simpler alternatives to ASIC management.

## 4. Post-Launch
- **Daily Review:** Monitor error logs and user feedback loops for the first 30 days.
- **Feature Roadmap:** Prioritize "Mystery Box" rewards and multi-currency support based on early user demand.
- **Customer Support:** Ensure 24/7 responsiveness for any payout or contract activation issues.

---

*Launch date targets: December 2026.*
